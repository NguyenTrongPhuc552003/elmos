# Code of Conduct

ELMOS adopts a professional, respectful collaboration model.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate directly and respectfully
- review technical work in good faith
- focus disagreement on design, evidence, and maintainability
- avoid harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and any other project space.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when
behavior harms the project or its contributors.
